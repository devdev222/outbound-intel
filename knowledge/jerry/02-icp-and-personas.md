# ICP and personas (placeholder)

The learned ICP profile in the scoring model is the source of truth for who
converts; this file holds the qualitative context Jerry adds on top.

## Who buys

- Manufacturers and distributors shipping 200 to 2,000 loads a month, usually 200 to 1,500 employees.
- Operations led by a VP of Transportation, VP of Supply Chain or Director of Logistics who owns both cost and service.
- A trigger is almost always present: a new facility, a hiring wave in coordination roles, a new ops leader, or a visible freight cost problem.

## Persona notes

- VP of Transportation: cares about service metrics and headcount plans. Talk in loads per coordinator and on-time percentage.
- Director of Logistics: closest to the pain. Talk about exceptions, tender rejections and the spreadsheet.
- COO: cares about scaling without adding fixed cost. Talk about the expansion plan.
- CFO: only engage when freight cost pressure is public. Talk in cost per shipment and quarter-over-quarter.
- Plant Manager and Procurement: influencers, rarely the buyer. Use for discovery, route the ask upward.

## Disqualifiers

- Fewer than 50 loads a month.
- Freight is almost entirely parcel.
- A recent managed-transportation contract signed within the last 12 months (ask).
