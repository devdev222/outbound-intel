# Objections and talk tracks (placeholder)

## Talk-track principles

- Open with what they did, not who we are: reference the exact page section, calculator input or video moment.
- One number, one question. Never three numbers.
- Offer a next step that takes less than 20 minutes.

## Common objections

**"We already have a TMS."**
Good. We work on top of it. The question is who is doing the tendering, tracking and exception work inside it, and whether that scales with the new facility.

**"We have a broker."**
Most of our customers did. The difference is a dedicated team and reporting on your lanes, not a load-by-load phone relationship. Ask what their tender acceptance rate is; if they do not know, that is the opening.

**"Not a priority right now."**
Tie back to the trigger: "You are hiring three coordinators and opening a site in Q1. The cheapest time to change the operating model is before they start."

**"Send me information."**
Send the personalized page link (it already has their numbers) and propose a specific 15-minute slot.

## Example talk track

"Hi {first name}, noticed you were exploring the ROI calculator; looks like you are shipping around {shipments} a month from {city}. Want me to walk through how teams at similar volume are saving 40+ hours a week?"
