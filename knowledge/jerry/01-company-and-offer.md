# Company and offer (placeholder)

Cartage provides managed transportation for shippers: capacity on contracted and
spot lanes, tendering, tracking, exception handling and freight audit, run by a
dedicated team on Cartage's platform. Customers keep their carriers if they want
to; Cartage adds coverage, automation and people.

## What we sell, in one sentence

Shipping operations that scale with volume instead of with coordinator headcount.

## Core outcomes we can credibly promise

- Coordination time: 30 to 45 hours per week back for a team shipping 300 to 800 loads a month.
- Service: on-time delivery in the mid-to-high 90s on managed lanes.
- Cost: typically 8 to 12 percent lower freight cost per shipment after the first full quarter, mostly from consolidation, better tender compliance and spot-market avoidance.

## What we do not do

- We are not a software-only TMS. If a prospect wants to self-serve software, we are the wrong fit.
- We do not promise savings on lanes with fewer than roughly 20 loads a month.
- We do not replace a shipper's existing carrier contracts unless asked.

## Proof points reps can use

- Case studies live in `lib/content/case-studies.ts` and on every personalized page.
- Implementation is two to three weeks for a single site; new sites added in under a week.
